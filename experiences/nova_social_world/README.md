# NOVA Social World — Central Plaza

A multiplayer 3D social environment where players gather, socialize, interact with monuments and points of interest, and broadcast messages.

## Mechanics
- **Central Fountain Hub**: Grand central gathering point with social fountain.
- **Player Spawning & Visible Avatars**: Multiple players synchronize positions and animations across the server.
- **Interaction Points**: Chat stands, information kiosks, and emote stages.
- **Player List HUD**: Real-time display of connected users.
- **Safe Chat Broadcasting**: Broadcast messages to everyone in the plaza instance.

## Testing & Verification
Multiplayer networking, room state replication, and player join/disconnect flows are tested and validated.
