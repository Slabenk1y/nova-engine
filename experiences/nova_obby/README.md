# NOVA Obby — Rainbow Tower Challenge

A colorful 3D obstacle-course game designed to test character movement, physics, moving obstacles, checkpoints, and level progression.

## Mechanics
- **Start Zone**: Safe starting platform with spawn checkpoint.
- **Section 1: Floating Step Stones**: Elevated platforms requiring precise jumps.
- **Section 2: Moving Slider Hazard**: Dynamic obstacle moving left and right across the path.
- **Section 3: Lava Run**: Floor covered with deadly lava triggers causing instant respawn.
- **Checkpoints**: Intermediate glowing platforms updating player respawn point.
- **Finish Gateway**: Golden victory gate recording completion time and awarding victory trophy.

## Controls
- **Virtual Joystick**: Walk and sprint.
- **Jump Button**: Jump over gaps and obstacles.
- **Camera Drag**: Orbit perspective camera.
