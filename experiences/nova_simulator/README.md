# NOVA Simulator — Crystal Miner

A progression-based mining simulator used to test game logic, player interactions, upgrades, and data persistence across sessions.

## Mechanics
- **Mining Nodes / Crystals**: Interactive 3D crystals scattered throughout the mine with finite health points.
- **Harvesting Interaction**: Clicking/touching crystals deals damage and harvests Nova Ore.
- **Backpack Capacity**: Players gather ore up to their backpack limit and sell it at the Sell Ring for Coins.
- **Upgrade Shop**: Purchase upgraded pickaxes and larger backpacks using Coins.
- **Persistent Progress**: Coins, equipment level, and stats are saved to the NOVA backend and restored across sessions.
